Nobody Cares

When a ceremony becomes a performance for everyone else.

Inspired by Haruki Murakami’s The Elephant Vanishes and the idea of the elephant in the room, this project stages a wedding in which the supposed protagonists gradually disappear from everyone’s attention.

Watch Nobody Cares on YouTube ↗

The stage is set

A wedding becomes a setting for competing social expectations. The elephant stands for the person whose own story is being overtaken.

Everybody has their own story

Animal guests bring their own interests to the celebration. Their images extend the narrative into ZOOCHAT, a fictional social feed.

ZOOCHAT

A fictional social feed from the wedding guests. Scroll inside the screen to follow the stories being told around the ceremony.
