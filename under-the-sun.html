Artist’s book · Graphic design

Under the Sun

We look at the sun. What if it looks back?

Under the Sun takes the form of a human observation report: an artist’s book that imagines the sun observing humanity. While we project myths, power, desire and emotion onto it, the sun continues to exist beyond those meanings. The project shifts the viewer from observer to observed.

A circle within a human frame

Rectangular pages hold the symbols and stories of human civilisation. Circular pages, coloured by abstracted solar imagery, turn independently at the centre. The book’s structure brings these two perspectives into physical contact.

The meanings we project

Religious imagery, flags, films, jewellery and everyday symbols show how the sun becomes a carrier of human belief and desire. Monochrome cultural images surround a vivid solar form.

Light, paper, reflection

The unfolding sphere and reflective surfaces change as the reader moves. Each view offers only a fragment, asking how much of nature we can ever claim to understand.
