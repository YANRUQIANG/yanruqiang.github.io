Artist’s book · Graphic design

Under the Sun

We give the sun meaning. The sun carries on.

An experimental book explores the distance between the sun as a natural phenomenon and the meanings people project onto it. A rotating paper sphere sits among images of religion, culture and everyday life.

A book that opens into a sun

The circular form unfolds from the flat page. Turning it changes the relationship between the solar imagery and the cultural images surrounding it.

Two ways of seeing

Saturated solar colours meet monochrome cultural imagery. Reflection and changes in viewpoint make the reader part of this encounter.

Light, paper, reflection

The physical book brings the research into a tactile form, using reflective surfaces and a sculptural centre.
