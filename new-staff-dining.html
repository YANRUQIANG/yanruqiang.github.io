Installation · Graphic design · Moving image

New Staff Dining

What are we really being asked to swallow?

A fictional staff restaurant turns the unspoken rules of the workplace into a meal. Food, distorted cutlery, ties and a collage table runner make hierarchy, flattery and coercion tangible.

A seat at the table

Familiar office gestures enter the dining room. The meal becomes a performance of the relationships people are expected to accept.

The language of obedience

A blue table runner brings together office equipment, fragments of the body and slogans about success. The everyday objects become a vocabulary of pressure.

Objects with an attitude

Distorted utensils translate discomfort and suppressed anger into objects held by the body.
