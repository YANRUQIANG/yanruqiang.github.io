Speculative design · Installation · Book design

Rewilding

How might we encounter the people we have lost?

Set in an imagined 2070, Rewilding proposes an urban mourning system connecting funeral rituals, water networks and ecological habitats. It asks how remembrance could restore a sense of reverence for nature — and how technology might shape that belief.

Last Words

A book records the natural form a person wishes to return to. Within this speculative system, it becomes a statement of intention and a tangible gift for those left behind.

An object for remembrance

A sculptural personal terminal connects the imagined habitat to a mourning ritual. Natural signs, from fireflies to birds, become ways of sensing a continued presence.

A space between presence and absence

The installation combines sculptural objects with a TouchDesigner environment. Light and interaction bring personal remembrance into a shared ecological landscape.
