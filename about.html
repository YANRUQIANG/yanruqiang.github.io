About

Yanru Qiang.

Thinking through images.
Making room for
another reading.

I work across graphic design, artist’s books, moving image and visual storytelling.

My personal projects look at the rules and rituals of everyday life: the pressure of the workplace, the performance of a wedding, and the meanings we give to the natural world.

Alongside this, my commercial work explores identity and visual communication through projects such as Rotpunkt.
