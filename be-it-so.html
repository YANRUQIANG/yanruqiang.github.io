Mr Platypus's Planet

On the Ching Ming Festival of 2024, I have thought long and hard and decided to write this. A gift to my lifelong friend Mr Platypus, and a sincere wish to all who read this, to cherish all the feelings we had while we were still alive.

The last time I saw the beast was two years ago, and even now I have no idea about Mr Platypus and the remote sanatorium where he and I last met, but it is as if his departure has invariably given me a sense of "mission", so please allow me to be sure to tell you all about a piece of his ordinary past.

Mr Platypus was my only close friend when I was studying at Yamano University. I remember the first time I met him, clad in a grey rocker jacket, black sweatpants printed with an unknown white embroidered label, and a pair of somewhat long-worn trainers underneath, he stood on the other side of the flowerbed designated for the distribution of new books, pretending to be swiping his phone to look for a message, while in fact occasionally shifting his eyes to the side of the flowerbed. Perhaps because the sun shone at the right place during the school year, I could clearly see the movement of his glittering black eyes from a distance. It's not unlike the first image of a platypus that appeared in Wikipedia when you searched for the word "platypus".

The beast's appearance is definitely not handsome, and his body is not tall nor does he have any visible fat exposed. Because of its docile nature and honest, slightly stupid appearance, it was described by a blabbering classmate as an illustration of a platypus printed in a secondary school biology textbook - an under-evolved, ungainly, ugly thing with a strange, convex beak that looked like it had been placed there. The beast responds with humour, saying, "Wizards that can be both on land and in water, oviparous and lactating, and have the ability to do both, have now disappeared from the earth." Like an open-minded human with six fingers, the beast's talkativeness is always unexpectedly shocking and heart-warming.

Our major was computer science, and the platypus, who didn't have any bad habits, was a bit of an exception among a portion of the boys who yelled at their immature half-knowledge and had a penchant for ranking the girls in the class late at night. The Beast's silent appearance was not exactly "very sophisticated"; after all, he was more like a pity party for many of the "ugly" ones. In the noisy men's dormitory, he seems to be closer to the one who has finished reading the tragedy of life, a piece of granite. I remember seeing this stone in a museum when I was a child; it was sealed in a glass display case.

Two months after entering the school near the winter, we joined the same table tennis interest club, and naturally know each other, they often drink beer together, sunshine. We chatted and learnt that in his primary and secondary school years, Beast had never been praised by his teachers for his excellent work. He said that he was truly an average type, and that in his class, he could meet people who did too much better than he did in everything. As he spoke, the beast's brownish-grey fur blew lightly in the sunlight, as if he had long ago drawn a line between himself and humans, a species that is "extremely motivated" and "likes to compete". It's a pity that I haven't asked, "How do you dissipate heat or take a bath?" Hahaha, that might have been a bit presumptuous, after all, it's not too far from asking "Are black people's teeth black? Perhaps for myself, who was in college at the time, the beast and I were much more similar than my classmates and I? The isolation of the species was completely forgotten. His father, who loved to read, told him a quote by Romero, and it was one of the things that convinced him the most: that true heroism is to love life even when you see it for what it is.

I always feel that the beast is aware of some of the truths that human beings ignore, and this sense of mystery makes me focus more and more on the macro world of "not in front of me", and the beast seems to be such a hero who is already well versed in the world of affairs, firm and great.

Later, in his third year of university, Mr Platypus fell in love with a girl in the next class. Until then, no one had ever noticed any signs of his behaviour, no irregular restlessness, and no one had ever found him dating a female student somewhere. Until suddenly, one evening, a bouquet of roses was placed on the beast's desk. The roommate was skeptical and tried to ask for the name of the girl she was dating. The beast's bright black eyes first looked out of the window, then sincerely smiled and looked straight at the other person, and spoke the questioning boy's heart has always been secretly in love with someone. As to how the beast knows the other person's crush, I guess it's what he calls "the second ability that the platypus has".

The Beast, then, had a normal relationship that began with "sending a bouquet of roses", although at a student union bonding event, the girls were asked by other members, "How did you have sex with a grey-haired, unassuming platypus?" And even more rashly, "Does Beast-kun have a penis like his mouth, like it's planted on it?" . But the girls laughed in disbelief. Over time, we found that the beast has a strong qualities that can be said to be well versed in this kind of creature of the girls, for example, in the face of the girlfriend's unreasonable speculation and temptation, the beast's surprisingly stable emotions and always correct attitude, so that the girls around the "legions" at the same time eating the beast hiked a long way to get the snacks at the same time, the beast of the yin and yang jokes! Girlfriends: "This beast from outer space, I wonder where he learnt the rules of mankind".

What is even more surprising is that there are a few extremely good-looking girls, just for the sake of being able to have sex with "strange beasts", jealousy, even said, "You are worthy of such a request with the platypus gentleman?" He said, "You're not worthy to make such a request with a platypus? According to Mr Beast, he went to the canteen alone one day, and in the queue for chicken fillet rice, a sexy girl in a sexy dress made eyes at him, and ended up sitting on his left side and stroking his fluffy arm. ......

I've completely forgotten the exact date when the girl grew cold to Mr Beast, only remembering losing touch from time to time until she finally disappeared completely, leaving Beast devastated. How could there be a force that would cause such pain to a "calm beast who knows the answers of humans"? Could this be what he called "destiny"?

The night I lost my ruby ring, I drank too much water, and stumbled upon the beast standing outside my dormitory door, writing symbols in the palm of his hand that I couldn't understand. Seeing that I had noticed his haste, he immediately collected a million thoughts that way, staring at me warily with strange black eyes and questioning me in a tone that forced me to calm down my mood about the reason I had risen in the night, presumably in an effort to make me forget about his strange behaviour. I flicked my eyes away to avoid his sharp gaze and simply replied, "Too much water."

For the first time I saw the Beast's eyes, which were kind and warm on a normal day, become this inscrutable deep, like two black black holes of immense energy that were going to swallow me whole and then instantly tear me apart into ash with its own immense density. In an instant, my mind began to go off on a tangent, remembering playing ping pong with the Beast as he looked around for the golden jumping round and picked it up to continue the game, his quiet selection of chicken breasts at the convenience store as he carefully considered the difference between curry flavour and the original, and the way the creases on his trouser legs changed direction forever as he prepared to sit down on the bus. I, who had never believed in the existence of a deity or a higher power, had a fear I had never known before. I was just about to run away when he tugged at me and said, "Wait, I'm a little thirsty, can I trouble you for a glass of water?" I turned my head quickly and crashed into the darkened bedchamber, feeling for the glass of water in the faint light. Stepping outside, I saw, only to see, the beast crouching on the railing, with a hint of exhaustion, thanking me. The black eyes immediately became gentle and helpless compared to a moment ago, and the strange words written on his hand were nowhere to be seen. He uttered the second sentence that struck me: everyone lives in the world with a thousand pains.

I realised then that the Beast had been cleaning the ruby ring that had been custom made with a girl at the sink when the slippery soap had made him miss, and he had flicked it straight into the deep drain, but instead of falling into immediate despair, the Beast had taken refuge in a kind of silent chaos. He tried to fish the ring out of the sewer with various tools, but every attempt ended in failure. In desperation, he could only watch as the ring disappeared into the depths of darkness.

The reticent Mr Platypus does not dare to ask her class for news of her for fear of public opinion, and is left to suffer the unjustified loss alone. The girl's unceremonious departure, for Mr Beast, is like a large machine that has lost a not-so-important part, clunking and clanking hard to run, there will always be some occasional error without any sign. It seemed to me as if the beast was caught in a mysterious and confusing vortex, and that he was no longer the "mind-reading beast" he once was, but the vortex itself was what I had seen in his eyes the night the ruby was lost.

From that moment on, it was as if he had awakened some long-lurking ability - the beast could actually accurately predict the whereabouts of a certain item, as if a mysterious power was guiding him. He first used this magical power to help one of his classmates, find his lost student card, and then later, in order not to cause unnecessary trouble, the beast with shock and confusion became more silent.

Soon it was graduation season, and the story of Beast and Ruby still continued. Beast's homework study is quite outstanding, originally is very good, fully capable of in the direction of computer science major to continue further education development. However, in that year, his major suddenly changed from "Computer" to "Ruby", which seemed to be extremely ridiculous. He had always been a rational person, but for an unspeakable reason, he stayed in this land. Just like facing a question that he already knew the answer to but still had to answer, Beast continued to search for the answer to Ruby in the place he had been familiar with for four years. Far from being as good as the beast, I was sent to work in another city because of luck and my mentor's recommendation, where I met my wife. When I got married in the church, the face of Mr Platypus came to my mind, and it was as if he was wishing me well from far away. The number of contacts with the beast, like most classmates who have been separated for many years, I finally experienced what my parents call "the goodbye and farewell of classmates".

......

When I saw Mr Platypus again, it was because of a class reunion, and I learnt from others that Mr Platypus had an extremely rare and terminal disease. Thinking about the time I spent at school together, I frowned and typed with a trembling hand to send a text message to him, whom I hadn't seen for many years.

When I found him, he was living in a quiet nursing home compound, and sitting next to him was the older brother he had never spoken of, looking very much like him, with bright black eyes and grey hair, only with a slightly wider head. The beast lay on his bed and smiled, telling me that he was trying to make sense of the strange changes that were happening to him. Really, as they say in biology textbooks, platypuses belong to the transitional stage between the mammalian and oviparous categories and are extraordinarily susceptible to illness. For example, during college he would always get a sudden high fever or cough up blood desperately. Some time ago, he noticed that his limbs were shrinking.

He shouted for me, standing by his bedside, to get closer to him and opened his mobile phone to show me the pictures he had recorded for nearly a month, his leathery flippers collapsing more and more with each passing day. I looked at his white sheet and could vaguely feel the dying lower limbs wrapped up in it, and I gently took his paws in both hands, and he felt and responded to me with a feeble but desperate effort of breath.

According to Beast, the year after graduation, he found the ring that was thrown into the abyss that year in a vintage shop in the city where he was working, using the intuition he had stumbled upon. And what surprised him even more was that the seller was the girl's new boyfriend. Confronted with this, the gentleman feels an inexplicable sense of relief and entanglement. Meanwhile, the man is the head of a mysterious religious organisation that holds the final destination of millions of lives. He was well aware of the man's true intentions, as his own mother had been separated from her family for similar reasons, but now he couldn't help the girl she had once spent so much time with at all, and that time had left him unable to open his mouth to utter a single word. He thought of One Hundred Years of Solitude, which he had read in his second year of college, and cried tears of sadness.

The last time he met with the girl, the beast asked her to meet him at an ordinary restaurant, he saw many handmade decorations with unintelligible tattoo patterns hanging on the girl's clothes, and chatted about a lot of irrelevant topics. About the girl's unceremonious departure in college, about the future of this city. The girl replied that she left because she met a man who knew more about what she needed spiritually and materially, and that the beast was just a mortal animal "enslaved" by the XX god who would only follow the "so-called right path" honestly. She also says "This is a city without hope, and the only way to get to the true light is to pray for the afterlife." "Beasts and any category will fall into slavery to the god of xx."

Before the meal ended, he handed the girl a small bag filled with a kraft bag containing all the savings he had accumulated from his part-time college job.

He did the best he could, and all the worry and prayers couldn't sway her choice.

The day I left, the beast slipped into a coma. Before I left I managed to summon up the courage to say to him, "The girl has sent you a letter of thanks, telling him that she has taken the plunge and left that organisation and has started her life afresh."

The work brigade at the sanatorium advised me not to stay long, and my friend as a platypus, the two-legged beast, fell back on extra urgency. As I left, I saw a group of beasts running neatly and quietly towards that ward, some with long beaks, some with brown fur, some with pointy eyes, and many, many more that didn't look like me.

This year, on 6 April 2024, my wife and I said we wanted to go to the place where Mr Platypus and I last spent time, to commemorate all those things that can never come back. When I got off the bus, I found that the place had long been razed to the ground, covered with weeds, new high-rise buildings had been built nearby, and a few workers were tapping away at their "destiny". 12 o'clock in the middle of the day, it was as if a group of creatures like that had never existed on this piece of land before.

My wife held my hand tightly and said, "There is nothing wrong with the shackles of the fingers, the restraints, the desires."
