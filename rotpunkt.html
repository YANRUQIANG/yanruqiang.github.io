Brand identity · Apparel · Art direction

Rotpunkt.

One more try
till Rotpunkt.

A visual identity rooted in climbing: chalk-covered hands, repeated attempts and the moment a route finally comes together. An expressive wordmark and a red-centred symbol carry that spirit across clothing, photography and campaign imagery.

A mark with movement

A compact symbol meets a restless, hand-drawn wordmark. Red cuts through black, grey and chalk white; the supporting graphics draw on routes, fingerprints and traces of the hand.

Worn, not just seen

The identity moves onto T-shirts, labels and embroidered denim. Changes in scale, texture and placement give the same mark a different presence on each surface.

In motion, on the wall

Monochrome movement studies and climbing photography place the identity in the physical world of the sport: the body, the wall and another attempt.

Raw / Wild / Bold

Social layouts and campaign imagery extend the visual language. The recurring line “One more try till Rotpunkt” brings the work back to the persistence behind climbing.
